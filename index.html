About Time with Midwife Safia

Our Story

Time with Midwife Safia is a Ghanaian Non-Governmental Organization dedicated to improving the reproductive and maternal health of girls, women, and pregnant women across Ghana. Founded on the belief that every woman deserves access to quality health information and support, we work tirelessly to bridge gaps in maternal health education.

Our Vision

We envision a Ghana where every girl and woman has comprehensive knowledge about her reproductive health, where pregnant women receive compassionate and evidence-based care, and where maternal mortality is eliminated through education, advocacy, and community engagement.

Why Safia?

Named after our founding midwife, Safia represents the compassion, expertise, and dedication we bring to every interaction. Safia's commitment to women's health inspired this organization to reach beyond individual clinics and touch entire communities.

Core Values

Integrity

We maintain the highest standards of honesty and ethical conduct in all our work and interactions.

Compassion

We approach every woman with empathy, understanding, and respect for her unique journey and circumstances.

Innovation

We continuously seek innovative ways to reach more women and deliver better health outcomes.

Community-Centered

We believe in the power of communities and involve them in all aspects of our work.

Evidence-Based

All our programs are grounded in scientific evidence and best practices in maternal health.

Equity & Justice

We work to ensure equitable access to health information and services for all women, regardless of background.

Meet Our Founder

Midwife Safia

Midwife Safia is a compassionate healthcare professional with over 15 years of experience in maternal and reproductive health. Her dedication to serving women in underserved communities inspired the creation of this organization. Safia's personal commitment to education and advocacy drives our mission every day.

Our Programs

School-Based Education

Reaching Young Girls

We conduct comprehensive sexuality education and reproductive health awareness sessions in secondary schools across Ghana. Our curriculum is age-appropriate, culturally sensitive, and empowers girls with knowledge about their bodies and health.

  • Puberty and menstruation education
  • Sexual health and consent
  • Contraception options
  • STI prevention
  • Peer support training

    • Prenatal Care Advocacy

    Supporting Pregnant Women

    We provide comprehensive prenatal education and advocacy programs for pregnant women and their families. Our goal is to ensure every pregnancy is wanted, every childbirth is safe, and every mother thrives.

  • Prenatal care guidelines
  • Nutrition during pregnancy
  • Antenatal exercise
  • Birth planning
  • Family involvement strategies

    • Community Awareness

    Engaging Entire Communities

    We organize community forums, health talks, and awareness campaigns to engage whole communities in maternal health advocacy. We work with men, elders, and community leaders to address cultural barriers.

  • Community health talks
  • Male engagement programs
  • Elder sensitization
  • Radio and media campaigns
  • Health fairs and exhibitions

    • Postpartum Support

    Caring for New Mothers

    Our postpartum program supports new mothers through the critical postpartum period. We provide education on breastfeeding, postpartum recovery, postnatal depression awareness, and infant care.

  • Breastfeeding support
  • Postpartum recovery guide
  • Mental health awareness
  • Infant care basics
  • Mother support groups

    • Adolescent Health

    Empowering Young Women

    Specifically designed for adolescent girls aged 10-19, this program addresses the unique health challenges faced by young women in their critical developmental years.

  • Puberty education
  • Menstrual health
  • Self-care practices
  • Relationship education
  • Career and health integration

    • Policy Advocacy

    Systemic Change

    We engage with government bodies, health institutions, and policymakers to advocate for improved maternal health policies, better healthcare access, and stronger support systems.

  • Policy briefs development
  • Government engagement
  • Health system strengthening
  • Research documentation
  • Legislative advocacy

    Training & Workshops

    We conduct training sessions and workshops for students, teachers, health workers, and community members on various aspects of maternal and reproductive health.

    We develop and distribute evidence-based educational materials including brochures, posters, and guides on reproductive and maternal health topics.

    Our trained counselors provide confidential and supportive counseling on reproductive health, pregnancy concerns, and related issues.

    We conduct research on maternal health challenges and solutions, contributing to evidence-based policymaking and program development.

    We collaborate with health institutions, NGOs, and government bodies to strengthen maternal health systems and expand our reach.
